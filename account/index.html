    Privacy

    What an account stores

    • Your username, and a salted, one-way hash of your password. Nobody can read your password back, including us.
    • Which roles you have (player, artist, admin), when you joined and when you last signed in.
    • Your game saves, for as long as you keep them.
    • Your email address only if you tick the updates box. Untick it and it's deleted, not archived.

    No real names, no IP addresses, no tracking. Signing in sets one cookie, which only keeps you signed in and is only ever sent to this site's account service. Delete your account and all of it goes.
