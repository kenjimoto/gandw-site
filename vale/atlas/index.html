    About the maps

    How it was put together

    Every coastline comes from the hand-drawn maps of Vale. The surveyed plate of Ometia sets where the land sits; the named Westerly and Easterly maps add the newer islands and the place names; the world sheet carries the O’Pinne. Names were read off those maps by eye, so a town can be out by fifteen miles or so.

    The world is an equirectangular sheet 29,400 miles around with a 22.5° axial tilt, so the tropics sit at 22.5° and the polar circles at 67.5°. The poles beyond the drawn sheet are ice. The climate layer is worked out from biome and latitude rather than drawn, and the O’Pinne shows nation centres only; its cities aren’t mapped yet.
