The world

The Vale Universe

Vale is the dying heart of Thea, Earth's sister, destroyed in our own ancient past. What survived is an endless ocean, a shattered geography, and Anima — the force that gives life to things that shouldn't have it.

Canon

One world, several doors

AbyssFall, A Vale Beyond and the Yarr system are all set here. So are the novels. The world is documented as a single body of work — cosmology and history, geography and biomes, peoples and factions, biology and ecology, material culture, and the systems that tie them together.

Atlas

The world, in the round

Vale is mapped the way a surveyor would want it: a terrain plate of Ometia, biome maps, a scale-and-distance chart, and named plates for the Easterly Kingdoms and the Westerly State. All of it is now wrapped onto one globe you can turn, with 170-odd named places, four surface layers and the Laputan storm track.

Language

The Valesian script

Vale has working languages, not decorative ones: phonologies for Annu, Nephilim and Theil–Dais, a linguistic history connecting them, a master lexicon, and a Rosetta document for the Barteeba stone.

The Valesian cipher exists as a real, installable typeface with a published character map. It is the kind of detail that takes a world from described to inhabited.
