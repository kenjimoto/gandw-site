    How it works

    Getting on the list

    1. Create a free account. A username and password are all it takes.
    2. Send the form with that username and an email address we can reply to.
    3. We'll add your account to the artist list and let you know.
    4. Sign in, read a brief, and press Earmark to claim it. Each req goes to one artist. If you can't do it after all, release it and it goes back on the list.

    The site never stores your email address. It travels inside the message to our inbox, so we can reply, and nowhere else.
