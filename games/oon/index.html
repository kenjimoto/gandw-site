The game

A maze pursuit game for two to four players

A custom 12×12 gridded board holds a maze of single-width corridors — every path exactly one cell wide, every corner reachable. Players navigate by rolling a die and drawing a direction card. The die tells you how far. The card tells you which way. The maze decides whether you like it or not.

Capture every rival by overtaking them on your turn. Last token standing wins.

Custom boards and pieces are encouraged.
