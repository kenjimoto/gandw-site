About Goggles & Windchimes

Goggles & Windchimes is an independent games and tabletop imprint founded by Ken Bewley, publishing browser games, PC games and tabletop roleplaying titles. Its catalogue spans absurdist digital design and the Vale Universe — a four-decade fantasy setting with its own ecology, history and constructed languages. The imprint published its first title, the maze pursuit game Oon, in 2025.
